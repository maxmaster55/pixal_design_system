Design language v1

Glowgrid · Ember theme

A pixel-art language for books, guides and pages about machines: a dark cavern lit from within by embers, crystals and glowing pools. Art is generated by code on a tiny grid and scaled up with hard edges, then given a soft bloom. Covers carry the full color. Interiors stay white for print, with pixel ink accents.

Principles

PrincipleWhat it means in practice
Light lives inside the worldEvery bright pixel is a light source (ember, crystal, pool, drip). Rock is dark except where those sources reach it, and their color tints it.
Hard pixels, soft lightArt pixels are always crisp squares. Only the glow is soft, added on top as light, never by blurring the art.
Small grid, big scaleScenes are drawn at about 120 art pixels wide, then scaled 4x on screen or 8x for print. Never draw at full resolution.
Ramps and dither, no gradientsShading steps through short palette ramps, mixed with a 4x4 ordered dither. A smooth CSS gradient on art is a bug.
Generated, seeded, repeatableEvery scene comes from a seed, so the same seed gives the same picture in the preview, the PDF and the animation.
Color on the cover, ink insideFull-bleed dark art on covers and openers only. Running pages are white with one accent, so they print cheaply and read easily.

Layout and pages

Print pages are A4 (210 by 297 mm) with 19 mm side margins, 19 mm top and 17 mm bottom, one column of about 70 characters. Every Part opens on a new page with a cave band bleeding off the top edge (24 percent of page width tall), a Silkscreen kicker, a big chapter title, a bold lead sentence and the contents list. Folios sit at the bottom in Silkscreen: section on the left, page number on the right. Screen pages use a void background, 16 px minimum gutters, and cards in the cave color with hard 8 px offset shadows.

Art engine

All art comes from one script, ember.js. It builds caves with a cellular automaton, lights them with colored point lights through dithered ramps, adds the objects, then upscales and blooms.

StepRule
1. GridCover 120 by 170 art pixels (A4 ratio). Band 120 by 34. Icons 7 by 7, one color.
2. CaveRandom fill 47 to 50 percent, 4 to 5 smoothing passes (a cell becomes rock with 5 or more rock neighbours, air with 3 or fewer). Carve chambers with ellipse masks for the title, the focal object and a pool basin.
3. LightEach light has position, radius, intensity and color. Brightness falls off as i / (1 + d²/r²). Rock pixels touching air get +0.28 so edges read.
4. ShadePick from the rock or air ramp with Bayer 4x4 dithering, then add the light's color at 22 percent on rock and 16 percent in air.
5. ObjectsOne focal object (crystal, chip, key) at the composition center; 12 to 16 embers drawn as 3x5 binary glyphs with a 4-step trail; a glowing pool along the bottom; a few drips under ledges.
6. UpscaleNearest neighbour only, 4x for screen, 8x for print.
7. BloomAdditive radial glows from emissive pixels plus 2 to 4 large halos. See Glow.

Glow

The mellow glow is light added on top of the pixels with the "lighter" blend. Each emissive pixel gets a radial gradient: full strength at the center, 45 percent at 35 percent of the radius, zero at the edge. Keep it gentle: if the pixels of a glyph stop being readable, the glow is too strong.

Animation guide

Pixel art moves on a held cadence: the scene updates 12 times a second and each frame is held, never tweened between pixels. Things move in whole art pixels. Light breathes slowly, fire flickers quickly, and nothing moves just to move. The still frame at time zero is the print frame, and anyone who asks for reduced motion gets that still frame.

MotionRule
Frame rate12 fps, held frames (steps() in CSS, frame stepping in canvas)
Ember rise1.8 to 4.8 art px per second, looping back to start; sideways wobble of ±1 px on a 4.8 s sine
Ember flickerSmooth noise at about 6 Hz; below 25 percent the ember drops to its dim color for that frame
Crystal pulseHalo strength 80 to 120 percent on a 3.9 s sine; one highlight pixel winks off every 0.25 s in turn
SparklesOrbit the focal object at 0.25 rad/s, blink out when noise is below 35 percent
Pool shimmerDither pattern slides 3 px per second; bubbles hop up 0 to 2 px twice a second
DripsHang 5 s, then fall with gravity (quadratic) for 2.5 s into the pool

Reveal / transitionDither or wipe dissolve, 8 steps over 400 ms to 1.6 s

Do and don't

Do

  • Keep one focal light per scene and let everything else be dimmer.
  • Use seeds and record them, so a cover can be redrawn exactly.
  • Check that glowing glyphs still read as pixels.
  • Keep running text in Plex Sans and code in VT323.
  • Print interiors on white; keep dark art to covers and bands.

Don't

  • Smooth or blur the art itself, or scale with bilinear filtering.
  • Add rounded corners, soft drop shadows or glassy gradients.
  • Use emissive colors for things that are not lights.
  • Animate between pixels or at 60 fps easing.
  • Copy characters, sprites or logos from existing games.
