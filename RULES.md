# Glowgrid design rules

Glowgrid is a pixel-art design system built on one idea: **the world is dark, and every bright pixel is a light source inside it.** Scenes are generated by code on a tiny grid, scaled up with hard edges, and given a soft additive bloom. Interfaces and print pages around the art use the same tokens, type and hard-edged components.

The system is theme-agnostic. A **theme** decides what the scene is (a cavern of embers, a forge, an ice cave, or anything you invent) and which colors are the lights. The rules below hold for every theme.

## 1. Principles

1. **Light lives inside the world.** Every bright pixel is a light. Surfaces are dark except where a light reaches them, and the light's color tints them.
2. **Hard pixels, soft light.** Art pixels are crisp squares. Only the glow is soft, added on top with the `lighter` blend. Never blur or smooth-scale the art.
3. **Small grid, big scale.** Draw scenes about 120 art pixels wide (portrait cover 120x170, landscape cover 170x120, band 120x34, icons 7x7). Upscale 4x on screen, 8x for print.
4. **Ramps and dither, no gradients.** Shade through short palette ramps with a 4x4 Bayer dither.
5. **One focal light per scene.** A single object (crystal, cog, lattice, chip) is the brightest thing. Everything else supports it.
6. **Seeded and repeatable.** Every scene comes from a seed. Record the seeds you ship.
7. **Color on dark, ink on paper.** Full-bleed dark art only on covers, heroes and section openers. Print interiors are white with one accent so they print cheaply.
8. **Original art only.** Never copy characters, sprites, logos or levels from existing games or brands.

## 2. Tokens

Tokens are semantic. Themes override the light colors; the dark world, paper and type stay shared unless a theme has a reason to change them. Defaults below are the Ember theme. Machine-readable copy: [`tokens.json`](tokens.json). CSS: [`core/glowgrid.css`](core/glowgrid.css).

| Token | Default | Role | Emissive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--bg` | #0B0714 | Deepest background, screen ground | no |
| `--surface` | #170D24 | Cards and panels on dark | no |
| `--stone` | #3B2A3F | Mid tone, dividers on dark | no |
| `--stone-lit` | #8A5A5A | Surfaces near a light | no |
| `--accent` | #FF8A2A | Primary light: labels, markers, sparks | yes |
| `--accent-hot` | #FFD36B | Titles on dark, hottest pixels | yes |
| `--accent-deep` | #B24D0A | The accent on paper: tags, notes | no |
| `--glow-1` | #7CF36B | Secondary light: pools, terminal text, success | yes |
| `--glow-2` | #E94BD6 | The focal object | yes |
| `--glow-3` | #4BC6E9 | Cold counter-light: kickers, links on dark | yes |
| `--ink` | #1C1226 | Text and code blocks on paper | no |

Stone ramp: 120A1A 1F1229 2C1A35 3B2A3F 5A3D52 8A5A5A C4825F. Air ramp: 0B0714 100A1B 150C22 1B1029 241433 2F1A44. Paper: white page, `--tint-accent` for worked examples, `--tint` (violet F4EFF6) for exercises, rule D9D0DE, soft ink 5B4A63.

**Emissive colors mean "this is a light".** Don't use them for anything else in art. In UI they mark the one thing that should draw the eye.

## 3. Type

All four faces are on Google Fonts under the OFL; .ttf copies are in [`fonts/`](fonts/).

| Role | Face | Print | Screen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cover / hero title | Pixelify Sans 700, hard 1-step drop shadow (`--title-shadow`) + soft accent halo | 54 to 72 pt | clamp(40px, 9vw, 96px) |
| Section / chapter title | Pixelify Sans 700, `text-wrap: balance` | 28 pt | 34px |
| Sub-head | Pixelify Sans 600, led by a 2-step accent square with a hard ink shadow | 15 pt | 20px |
| Labels, kickers, page numbers | Silkscreen, uppercase, letter-spacing .12em to .3em | 7 to 9 pt | 11 to 12px |
| Running text | IBM Plex Sans 400/600, leading 1.5 | 10.5 pt | 15 to 16px |
| Code, numbers, terminal | VT323 at 1.25x body size | 12.5 pt | 18 to 22px |

Never set paragraphs in pixel type.

## 4. Layout and components

- **Screen:** `--bg` background, 16 px minimum gutters, cards in `--surface` with hard 8 px black offset shadows. No rounded corners, no soft drop shadows.
- **Print:** A4 portrait (one column, about 70 characters, margins 19/19/17 mm) or A4 landscape with two columns (margins 12/14/15 mm) to save paper. Each part opens on a new page with a scene band bleeding off the top (24 percent of page width), a Silkscreen kicker, a big title, a bold lead and a contents list with dotted leaders. Page numbers in Silkscreen at the bottom.
- **Pixel corners:** stepped clip-path, class `.px`.
- **Code blocks:** on ink, keywords `--accent-hot`, numbers `--glow-1`, comments muted violet.
- **Panels:** worked example (accent tint, gear icon), exercise (violet tint, question icon, answers below a dashed rule), tip, pitfall, terminal.
- **Tables:** ink header row in Silkscreen caps, 1 px rules.
- **Icons:** 7x7 one-bit (gear, q, key, chip, bug, skull, warn, book), drawn by the engine.
- **Buttons:** accent fill, hard 4 px shadow; hover lifts 2 px and grows the shadow to 6 px; press sinks 2 px.

## 5. Art pipeline

The engine ([`core/glowgrid.js`](core/glowgrid.js)) does this; themes reuse the steps and swap the objects.

1. **Terrain:** cellular automaton. Random fill 47 to 50 percent, 4 to 5 passes (solid if 5+ solid neighbours, air if 3 or fewer). Carve ellipse chambers for the title, the focal object and a pool, re-applied after each pass.
2. **Light:** point lights with position, radius, intensity and color; falloff i / (1 + d²/r²). Solid pixels touching air get +0.28 brightness.
3. **Shade:** pick from the stone or air ramp with Bayer dither, then add light color at 22 percent on stone, 16 percent in air.
4. **Objects:** one focal object at the center; 12 to 16 small sparks (embers, sigils, glyphs) with a short trail fading through darker steps of their color; a glowing pool along the bottom with a foam line, dithered depth and bubbles; drips under ledges; a few sparkles orbiting the focal object.
5. **Upscale:** nearest neighbour only (`imageSmoothingEnabled = false`, CSS `image-rendering: pixelated`).
6. **Bloom:** with `globalCompositeOperation = "lighter"`, draw a radial gradient per emissive pixel (alpha at 0, 45 percent at 0.35, 0 at 1) plus 2 to 4 big halos.

### Glow strengths (keep them mellow)

| Source | Radius (art px) | Peak alpha | Big halo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spark / hot spark | 6 | 0.06 / 0.07 | none |
| White highlight | 7 | 0.10 | none |
| Focal object | 4 to 6 | 0.01 to 0.03 | r 34 at 0.22 + core r 12 at 0.10 |
| Pool | 4 to 5 | 0.012 to 0.03 | r 46 at 0.12 |
| Drip | 5 | 0.08 | none |
| Title chamber | none | none | r 40 at 0.10 in the accent |

Test: if a glowing glyph stops reading as pixels, the glow is too strong.

## 6. Motion

- **Cadence:** 12 fps with held frames. Step time as floor(elapsed / (1000/12)) and redraw only on a new step; in CSS use `steps()`. Move in whole art pixels, never tween between them.
- **Sparks rise** 1.8 to 4.8 art px/s, looping, with a ±1 px sideways wobble on a 4.8 s sine. **Flicker** with smooth noise at about 6 Hz; below 0.25 show the dim color for that frame.
- **Focal pulse:** halo 80 to 120 percent on a 3.9 s sine; highlight pixels wink off one at a time every 0.25 s. **Sparkles** orbit at 0.25 rad/s.
- **Pool shimmer:** dither slides 3 px/s; bubbles hop 0 to 2 px twice a second. **Drips** hang 5 s, then fall with gravity over 2.5 s.
- **UI:** hover `transition: transform 80ms steps(2)`. Reveals and page transitions are dither or wipe dissolves, 8 steps, 400 ms to 1.6 s. Terminal text types at about 10 characters per second with a 1 Hz block cursor. Loading is an 8-cell spark chase.
- **Reduced motion and print:** render the still frame at t = 0 and switch animations off under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## 7. Making a new theme

1. **Pick a world and one focal light.** Examples: a server room lit by one status LED, a deep-sea trench with an anglerfish lure, a library lit by one lantern. Keep the dark, dithered terrain.
2. **Choose the lights.** Set `--accent`, `--accent-hot`, `--accent-deep`, `--glow-1..3` and `--tint-accent` in `themes/<name>.css`. Keep one warm and one cold light so scenes have contrast.
3. **Write the scene painters** in `themes/<name>.js`: copy `themes/frost.js`, change the palette, register the new colors in `Glowgrid.EMIT` (`[radius, alpha]`), draw the focal object and sparks, then call `Glowgrid.register("<name>", { cover, band })`.
4. **Pick sigils** for the sparks that fit the subject (binary digits, `& ? !`, `λ { } =`), drawn as 3x5 one-bit glyphs.
5. **Record seeds** for the covers and bands you ship in `tokens.json`.

## 8. Making a PDF

Lay out pages in HTML with `@page { size: A4; margin: 0 }` (or `A4 landscape`), draw art with the engine, wait for `document.fonts.ready` and the canvases, then print with Playwright: `page.pdf({ path, preferCSSPageSize: true, printBackground: true })`. Use the still frame. Render each part as its own PDF and merge them, because anything that bleeds above a forced page break gets painted on the previous page. A working pipeline is in [`examples/nixos-field-book/`](examples/nixos-field-book/).

## 9. Do and don't

- **Do** keep one focal light per scene, record seeds, keep text in Plex Sans and code in VT323, print interiors on white, and check pages as screenshots before shipping.
- **Don't** blur the art, use rounded corners or soft shadows, use emissive colors for things that aren't lights, animate with 60 fps easing, or copy assets from existing games.
