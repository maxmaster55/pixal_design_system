Motion: springs on the pixel grid

Things that move through space ride a spring: they keep momentum, can overshoot, and can change direction mid-flight. Each frame is then snapped to the 2 px grid, so a bounce is a few whole-pixel hops. Physics by Weave; the approach follows Material 3 Expressive (springs for space, short steps for colour).
